Wreckterra: Midtown map data notice
===================================

What this folder holds
----------------------
source.osm.json  The pinned map snapshot the city is compiled from. Its "wreckterra" header records
                 the kind of snapshot, where it came from (URL), when it was retrieved, the query,
                 and a SHA-256 of the "elements" array, which a test re-computes.
city.json        The game's city file, compiled from source.osm.json by src/maps/city-compiler.ts.
                 It is deterministic: the same snapshot always gives the same bytes.

The game shows "OpenStreetMap contributors" on the page while the city is on screen.

CURRENT STATE OF THE SNAPSHOT: SYNTHETIC STAND-IN
-------------------------------------------------
The snapshot committed with this change is NOT OpenStreetMap data. Overpass could not be reached
from the sandbox that built it, so `node scripts/acquire-midtown.mjs --stand-in` wrote a generated
street grid (rotated about 29 degrees, Central Park South to West 56th Street, Sixth Avenue to
Fifth Avenue, with a park edge and generated building lots) in the same JSON shape. Its header says
kind "synthetic-stand-in", its ids are negative, and it has no URL. It is original to this
repository and carries no third-party licence.

To replace it with the real extract, run on a networked machine:

    node scripts/acquire-midtown.mjs

That downloads the Overpass extract for the bounding box in the script, pins its URL, retrieval date
and SHA-256 into source.osm.json, and recompiles city.json. Use `--compile-only` to recompile from
the pinned file and `--skip-invalid` to drop ways with bad geometry (they are listed in
city.json under "rejected"). Production never calls Overpass, Nominatim or a tile server.
