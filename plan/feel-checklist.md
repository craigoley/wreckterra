# Wreckterra feel checklist

Every build is judged against these lines before it merges, in addition to its tests. The build's pull request says how it meets each one, or why a line does not apply yet.

1. **Something to fight in the first 10 seconds.** A new match puts enemies in view and in range quickly.
2. **Speed and handling.** Cars accelerate hard, turn tight, drift and recover; ramps and collisions feel physical, not floaty.
3. **Hits land.** Every hit shows feedback (flash, sparks, sound cue or shake); a missile lock is obvious.
4. **Danger is real.** Bots chase, fire and use pickups; standing still gets you wrecked.
5. **Readable damage.** Health is always visible; cars smoke when low and explode when wrecked.
6. **Short matches.** A match is won or lost in under 5 minutes, and restart is one tap.
7. **Phone first.** It plays on an iPhone in Safari at a steady frame rate with touch controls.
8. **Original.** Our own cars, drivers, names, arenas and art; nothing copied from an existing game.
